Accounts

    Adding an account here only names it. Each app still signs in to it with atproto OAuth, and the account's PDS has the final say.

    What links here

      Each app answers for itself, and only to the shell, so private notes stay private.

      Workspaces

      "Reading & notes" opens mock versions of Skyreader, Reams and Roomy, with fake data, to show actions and "what links here" across apps. Pick an account in the sidebar to start.

      Frames

      The demo app opens twice, as "Posts" and "Profiles". A link opened in one shows up in the other. Open the same URL twice to see two frames of one app share a storage partition.
